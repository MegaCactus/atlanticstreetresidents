355 Atlantic Street maintenance record

A residents’ record of maintenance at 355 Atlantic Street, Stamford. Building notices from October 2020 to the present, plus resident-submitted tickets.

Compiled from the building’s own email notices and from maintenance tickets residents have shared. Nothing here identifies a unit or a person.

Every incident since October 2020

One row per category, one mark per incident. Tall marks are unplanned failures or emergency work; short marks are scheduled shutoffs, inspections, and repairs; faint marks are informational notices. A wider mark spans multiple days.

Days affected, month by month

Each square is a day. In the water view, solid blue is a day with an unplanned outage of water or hot water (from a building notice or a boiler failure) and lighter blue is a scheduled shutoff. The other views color each day by its most relevant category. Where the building never announced a restoration, the incident is assumed to have ended the same day; the faintest squares are days inside an estimated end. Informational notices are left out.

Where in the building

The tower, floor by floor. Each residential floor is shaded by how many incident-days have touched it; a building-wide outage counts on every floor, a floors 17–26 outage only on those. The dashed line is where the hot water risers split, which is why so many notices name one half of the building. Click a floor to narrow the log below to it.

Where the problems are

Incidents by category. The solid part of each bar is unplanned; the light part is scheduled work and notices.

Hot water and plumbing incidents per year; the number inside each bar is how many were unplanned. 2020 covers October onward and 2026 runs through early September.

Resident tickets

Maintenance requests residents have shared from the building app, oldest first. Dates are shown by month and locations by floor range so that no ticket can be traced to a unit.

    Management changed hands three times in this period: Greystar (for owner RXR) in 2020, AVE from January 2022, Greystar again by mid-2022, and Bozzuto since June 2025.

    Method: every notice from the building’s notification system and its management addresses was grouped into incidents, so a multi-day outage with six update emails counts once. Floors and durations come from the full text of the notices. Rent reminders, events, holiday hours, and parking enforcement are left out. Resident tickets are shown by month and floor range only, with no tech notes, photos, or unit numbers.
