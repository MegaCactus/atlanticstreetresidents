          A note about the people who keep this building running

          The residents behind this record appreciate the hard work of the building's staff, the maintenance team and the concierge desk in particular, who show up for every one of the incidents listed here and many that never make a notice.

          This site exists for informational purposes only. Its hope is simple: that awareness of how often things go wrong, and for how long, brings improved living conditions for everyone in the building.

          Methods and limitations

          Where the data comes from

          Three sources feed this record. The first is the building's own email notices: the messages the building's notification system sends residents, grouped into incidents so that a multi-day outage with six update emails counts once. New notices are checked automatically as they arrive. Outages, repairs and planned work are added, with the category, type and affected floors read from the notice; rent reminders, parking warnings, events and other announcements are left out. The second is residents themselves, through the tickets and reports submitted on this site or emailed to it. The third is the residents' Discord, a chat that residents started during the February 2026 shutoff: its history from February to September 2026 was read through, and the problems residents described there were summarized into entries, each noting how many people reported it. Photos from the chat were included only where they show the problem itself.

          How things are counted

          • An incident is an “outage” when the building called it an emergency, an outage, a failure, or a leak, and “planned work” when it was announced in advance. Everything else is an announcement.
          • Floors come from the notice. When a notice named no floors, the incident is counted for the whole building.
          • An outage stays “still going” until the building announces it is fixed, or a resident or the site's editor marks it resolved. Water and heat outages with no restoration notice are assumed to have ended the same day, and end dates marked “about” are estimates.
          • Durations for water incidents come from the timestamps of the building's own emails. Where the building said cold water was back but hot water was not, the hot water date is the one used.

          What to keep in mind

          Resident tickets and reports are self-reported. They describe lived experience in the words of the person who submitted them and are not verified by the building or by anyone else. They can contain mistakes, duplicates, and opinions, and they are reviewed only for relevance and privacy before they appear here, not for accuracy.

          Entries drawn from the residents' Discord are summaries of what people said in the chat, with names, unit numbers, and anything identifying removed. Where the chat described an event the building had also announced, the entry appears as a report alongside the notice rather than as a second incident, so nothing is counted twice.

          The building's own notices on file go back to late 2020; anything earlier appears only where residents have added it.

          Building notices are summarized, not reproduced. The automatic reading of new notices can file something under the wrong category, miss the floors, or leave out a notice that mattered; those errors are corrected as they are noticed.

          This site is run by residents and has no connection to the building's ownership or management. Corrections and missed notices are welcome through the report form.

          Management changed hands three times in this period: Greystar (for owner RXR) in 2020, AVE from January 2022, Greystar again by mid-2022, and Bozzuto since June 2025.

          Photos submitted with tickets and reports are re-encoded in the browser before upload, which removes location and device data.
